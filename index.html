Who We Are

Daraja Academy is a fully sponsored, all-girls boarding secondary school located in Naibor, near Nanyuki in Laikipia County, Kenya. Founded in 2009, the school provides free, high-quality education and comprehensive support to its students, empowering them to become future leaders.

Our History

The academy officially opened its doors in 2009.

The visionary founders behind this initiative are Jenni and Jason Doherty.
