Daraja Girls High School

it was started in 2009

the founders are jenny and jason

a fully sponsored, all-girls boarding secondary school located in Naibor, near Nanyuki in Laikipia County, Kenya.
Founded in 2009, the school provides free, high-quality education and comprehensive scholarships to exceptional Kenyan girls born into extreme poverty.
The name "Daraja" means bridge in Swahili, symbolizing the school’s mission to serve as a bridge out of poverty through education, empowerment, and leadership development.
